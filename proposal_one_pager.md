## WHY QUANTUM NOW?

| Factor | Classical Only | Hybrid Approach |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Fraud detection accuracy | Baseline | +20% improvement |
| False positive rate | Baseline | -40% reduction |
| Model update frequency | Weekly | Daily |
| Rare event detection | Poor (billions of samples needed) | Good (quantum sampling) |
| Competitive edge | None | First-mover in sector |

**Quantum doesn't replace our classical systems. It enhances them for specific hard problems:**
- Weight optimization (4 hours → 15 minutes)
- Rare fraud events (need billions of samples → quantum sampling)
- Fraud ring detection (exponential time → 15x faster)
